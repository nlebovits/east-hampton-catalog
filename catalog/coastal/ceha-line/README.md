# Coastal Erosion Hazard Area Line

The Coastal Erosion Hazard Area (CEHA) line, 61 segments. CEHA is the State designation under Article 34 of the Environmental Conservation Law and 6 NYCRR Part 505. The Town's local erosion law is [Appendix G](https://dos.ny.gov/town-east-hampton-appendix-g-local-erosion-law) of its Local Waterfront Revitalization Program. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the column meanings and tested queries.
