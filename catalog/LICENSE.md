# Terms of use

This catalog mirrors layers from the Town of East Hampton, New York
[MapServiceForOpenGov](https://eh-gis.ehamptonny.gov/arcgis/rest/services/MapServiceForOpenGov/MapServer)
ArcGIS service. The Town publishes no license for this service. The catalog
declares the license `other` for that reason, and this page records the only
terms the source states.

## The Town's terms

The service carries this disclaimer in its `copyrightText` field. It is
quoted verbatim:

> WHILE EVERY EFFORT HAS BEEN MADE TO PROVIDE CURRENT AND ACCURATE
> INFORMATION, THE TOWN OF EAST HAMPTON MAKES NO REPRESENTATIONS AS TO
> ACCURACY, COMPLETENESS, CURRENTNESS, SUITABILITY, OR VALIDITY OF ANY
> INFORMATION ON THIS SITE AND WILL NOT BE LIABLE FOR ANY ERRORS, OMISSIONS,
> OR DELAYS IN THIS INFORMATION OR ANY LOSSES, INJURIES, OR DAMAGES ARISING
> FROM ITS DISPLAY OR USE. ALL INFORMATION IS PROVIDED ON AN AS-IS BASIS.

The service description also states:

> This is a map service for the express intent of being used in the Town's
> Open Gov application. It is designed specifically for that purpose and thus
> may not be optimal for use outside of that environment. Additionally there
> is some draft data included in the service that is used by staff, but is
> not however otherwise published or completed. Therefore this service
> contains draft data that may be incomplete.

Nothing in this catalog is an official Town record. The Town Code and the
Town's officially adopted maps govern zoning, overlay districts, and wetland
determinations.

## Layers this mirror leaves out

The mirror omits these layers on purpose:

- **EHTaxMap and AHO & LBO**, and the parcel-shaped Town property layers.
  The service credits the parcel linework as "COPYRIGHT, COUNTY OF SUFFOLK,
  N.Y. Real Property Taxmap parcel linework used with permission of Suffolk
  County Real Property Tax Service Agency (R.P.T.S.A.)". That permission
  covers the Town, not a third party. Suffolk County publishes its own
  [Tax Parcel](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=e3aeb4e052bb49ebaffe0dc12306b9e4)
  service under CC BY.
- **Building Footprints and Pool Points.** The layer descriptions name the
  Pictometry Building Outline Creation and PoolFinder products. Published
  EagleView (Pictometry) license terms limit derived data layers to the
  customer's internal use.

## Data from other agencies

Some collections are Town copies of data that another agency produces. Each
collection names the original agency as its producer and links the
authoritative dataset. Data that a U.S. federal agency produces (FEMA,
the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service, and USDA NRCS) is generally not subject
to copyright under [17 U.S.C. § 105](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/105).
New York State DEC and Peconic Estuary Partnership data carry the terms of
those publishers.
