# Special Groundwater Protection Areas

Special Groundwater Protection Areas (SGPA) in and near the Town. New York State designated the Long Island SGPAs under [ECL § 55-0113](https://law.justia.com/codes/new-york/2013/env/article-55/55-0113). They include the "South Fork Morainal Forest" and "the Hither Hills area of the town of East Hampton". SGPAs are not Town zoning districts. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the column meanings and tested queries.
