# Hydrogeologic Zone V

Hydrogeologic Zone V, one polygon in the west of the Town. The Town's Local Waterfront Revitalization Program ([Section XII](https://dos.ny.gov/town-east-hampton-section-xii-water-resources-policies-30-40-44)) describes Zone V as a "deep flow recharge" zone under Article 7 of the Suffolk County Sanitary Code. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the column meanings and tested queries.
